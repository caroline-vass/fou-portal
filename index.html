The three qualifying gates

Does your project qualify?

All three gates must be passed. Routine development, maintenance, and support are explicitly excluded (Prop. 2013/14:1 s.521–522).

01🏢

Commercial purpose

Conducted within a commercial business (näringsverksamhet). Internal research for your own products counts.

§3 lag om FoU-avdrag
02🔬

Systematic & qualified

Systematic research using scientific methods. Ad-hoc fixes or routine iteration do not qualify.

§4 lag om FoU-avdrag
03💡

New knowledge or product

Creates new knowledge, or a substantially new/improved product. Research must be the decisive foundation.

§4 lag om FoU-avdrag

Audit readiness

Documentation checklist

Tick each item off as you prepare it. Skatteverket can request documentation for any claimed period — have everything below in place before you file on AGI.

⚠ Two rules from CauseyWestling & Lag (2023:747) that catch people out: The ≥50% time threshold and the ≥15 hours/month floor are two separate conditions that must both be met in the same month. A person working 20 hours total at 100% R&D fails (under 15h). A person working 160 hours at 40% R&D also fails (under 50%). Also: if someone misses the threshold one month but their tasks haven't changed, they may still qualify under the continuity rule. Document both carefully.

The 3 qualifying gates (§3–5 lag om FoU-avdrag)

A project must pass ALL three to qualify. Routine development, maintenance, and support are explicitly excluded.

01
🏢

Commercial Purpose

The R&D must be conducted in the course of a business activity (näringsverksamhet). Internal research for your own products or services counts.

02
🔬

Systematic & Qualified

Work must be systematic research or development using scientific methods. Ad-hoc bug fixing or routine iteration does not qualify.

03
💡

New Knowledge or Product

Must create new knowledge, or a substantially new or improved product/process. The work must use research results as a decisive foundation.
